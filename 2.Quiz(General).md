**Question 24.**

1. $E[Y^2] - (E[Y])^2$
2. $E[X^2] - (E[X])^2 + E[Y^2] - (E[Y])^2$
3. $(E[X^2] - (E[X])^2) \cdot (E[Y^2] - (E[Y])^2)$
4. $E(XY) - E(X)E(Y)$

Consider again $X \sim N(1, 5^2)$ and $Y \sim N(-2, 3^2)$, but this time $X$ and $Y$ are _not_ independent. Then $Z = X + Y$ is still normally distributed with the same mean found in Question 23. What is the variance of $Z$ if $E(XY) = -5$?
